Two piles, not one

Core pile — about 20 papers. The best ones. We download the PDF and read them properly.

Wide pile — a few hundred. Title and abstract only. Never read in full.

Stage 2 needs both. The core pile is where ideas come from. The wide pile is what proves an idea has already been done.

What “top” means
Must have — a real journal or conference, confirmed in two places
Must have — not retracted
Helps — well-known venue in this field
Helps — cited often for its age and field
Helps — recent
Helps — the full text is free to read
Four things to fix in what you have now

1. Matching is done by counting shared words. A paper about “server room chilled water” will not match a topic written as “data centre cooling”. This is half the score right now. Use meaning-based matching instead.

2. It never follows references. This is the biggest miss. The fastest way to find the right papers is to look at what a good paper cites, and who cites it. Both sources give you this for free.

3. It does not check for retractions. A withdrawn paper could quietly become the basis of a research gap.

4. It punishes preprints even when they were later published. An arXiv paper that came out in a journal two years later should count as the journal version.
